I’m an incoming undergraduate student at the University of Washington, starting in Fall 2026, and I plan to major in Computer Science. I graduated from Shanghai Pinghe School, where I studied in the IBDP program and spent three years involved in robotics.

I’m particularly passionate about Robotics and Computer Vision.

## Experience
- As a team leader in First Robotics Competition (FRC), I worked across both the software and hardware sides of robotics development.

- On the software side, I implemented and tuned PID control systems, developed motion logic for autonomous behavior, and iteratively debugged control performance under real-world constraints. I also worked on robot path adjustment and system logic design to improve stability and responsiveness during operation.

- On the hardware side, I designed robotic structures and assemblies using SolidWorks, built physical prototypes through fabrication and iterative testing, and integrated sensors, motors, and microcontrollers through wiring and soldering. I participated in full system assembly and operational validation during testing and competition preparation.
